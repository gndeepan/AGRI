## What AWD is
Alternate wetting and drying is a water-saving technique for irrigated lowland rice. The field is allowed to dry for some days after the ponded water disappears, and then it is re-flooded.

## Safe AWD threshold
A perforated field-water tube shows the water level below the soil surface. In "safe AWD" the field is re-flooded to about 5 cm when the water level falls to about 15 cm below the soil surface. Applied this way, AWD does not reduce yield.

## When not to use AWD
Keep ponded water from about one week before flowering until one week after flowering, because the crop is most sensitive to water stress around flowering. Weed control is easier if the field stays flooded for the first 2–3 weeks after transplanting.

## Before harvest
Draining the field about 10–15 days before the expected harvest helps the crop ripen evenly and makes harvesting easier.
