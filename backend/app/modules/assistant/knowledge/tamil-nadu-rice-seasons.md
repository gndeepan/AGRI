## Seasons
Tamil Nadu has several rice seasons. Kuruvai is sown around June–July with short-duration varieties. Samba is sown around August with long-duration varieties. Thaladi is sown around September–October with medium-duration varieties, often after a Kuruvai crop. Navarai is sown around December–January with short-duration varieties. Exact windows differ by district, so check the local advisory.

## Duration groups
Short-duration varieties take about 105–115 days, medium about 125–135 days and long about 145–160 days. Examples listed by TNAU are ADT 43, ADT 45, CO 51, ADT 53 and ASD 16 (short), TKM 13 (medium), and BPT 5204 and CR 1009 Sub1 (long, Samba).
