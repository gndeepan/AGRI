## Soil Health Card
The Soil Health Card scheme gives farmers a report on their soil's nutrient status: N, P and K, secondary nutrients, micronutrients, pH, EC and organic carbon. It comes with crop-wise fertilizer recommendations. Contact the local Agriculture Department office or a soil-testing laboratory to have your soil tested.

## Why soil tests matter
Fertilizer needs differ from field to field. Gridded soil maps such as SoilGrids are coarse model estimates and cannot replace a laboratory test of your own field.

## Split application in rice
For rice, nitrogen is usually split into a basal dose and top-dressings at active tillering and at panicle initiation. A leaf colour chart (LCC) can help time nitrogen top-dressing to what the crop needs. Take the actual quantities from your soil-test-based recommendation or your Agricultural Officer.
