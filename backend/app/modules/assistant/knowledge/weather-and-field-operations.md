## District agro-advisories
IMD and its partner agro-met field units publish district-level agro-advisories twice a week. They advise on field operations based on the medium-range forecast. These advisories and IMD warnings are the official sources.

## Rain and spraying
Avoid applying fertilizer or pesticide when heavy rain is expected within about 24 hours. Rain can wash products away and pollute water.

## Wind
Avoid spraying in strong wind because of drift. During strong wind and rain, mature crops are at risk of lodging, so check drainage and bunds.
