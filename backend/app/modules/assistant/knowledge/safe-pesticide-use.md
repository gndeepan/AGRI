## Use only registered products as labelled
Use only products registered for the crop and pest concerned. Follow the label and leaflet for dose, dilution, waiting period and safety. Do not mix products unless the label allows it.

## Protect yourself
Wear gloves, a mask and full-sleeve clothing while mixing and spraying. Do not spray against the wind, and do not eat, drink or smoke while spraying. Store chemicals away from children, food and water sources.

## Get advice first
Contact the local Agricultural Officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) before using a chemical. They will check whether the pest has crossed the economic threshold and recommend an approved product.
